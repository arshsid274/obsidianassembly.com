OriginObjects

Origin Objects are physical items formed within specific places.

Their form results from context, material, and use at the point of origin.

Principle

Objects are not designed first and placed later.

Objects are not designed first and placed later. They are formed within a place and only leave once complete.

After release, they circulate independently.

Each object is tied to a specific place, which defines its origin.
